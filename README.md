# Movie Business Analytics & Investment Decision System

A data-driven business analytics project that evaluates movie financial performance and translates historical data into investment-oriented insights, risk assessment, capital allocation scenarios, and management recommendations.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes movie-level financial data to answer a practical management question:

> **Given historical financial performance, which investment opportunities appear attractive, what risks should management consider, and how could a limited investment budget be allocated?**

The project combines financial KPI analysis, profitability and ROI analysis, historical performance trends, outlier-aware analysis, investment scoring, scenario analysis, and dashboard visualization.

## Business Problem

Investment decisions based only on revenue can be misleading. A movie may generate high revenue but require a very large budget, while extremely high ROI values can sometimes be driven by unusually small budgets.

This project therefore evaluates opportunities using multiple business metrics:

- Budget
- Revenue
- Profit
- ROI
- Profit Margin
- Historical Performance
- Investment Risk
- Investment Score
- Capital Allocation Scenarios
